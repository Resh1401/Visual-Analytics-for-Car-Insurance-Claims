# Visual Analytics for Car Insurance Claims

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d8112066-6863-4be7-aa4e-302f4f314215)

1. Project Objectives:

* Analyze accident and claim data to identify trends, anomalies, and risk factors
* Build dashboards to explore claim frequency, policy types, customer demographics, and fraudulent behavior
* Develop an interactive storyboard that enables business leaders to derive actionable insights
* Recommend strategic decisions to optimize claim processing and improve customer satisfaction

2. Key Highlights:
   
* Created 5+ interactive dashboards illustrating accident frequency, average claim amounts, regional claim distribution, and customer segmentation
* Developed calculated fields to categorize drivers by age group and analyze claims based on accident severity
* Enabled parameter-based filtering for dynamic analysis of education level and vehicle type impact on claims
* Built a dedicated Executive Summary Dashboard to present key findings and recommendations for senior leadership
* Final storyboard aligned with real-world business objectives to improve underwriting, detect fraud, and streamline claims operations
